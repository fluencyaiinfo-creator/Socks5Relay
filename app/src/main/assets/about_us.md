# About Us

## Who we are

VERTEX TECH SOLUTIONS builds Socks5 Relay – VPN & Proxy, a lightweight network utility for Android that lets you connect to and test SOCKS5, SOCKS4, and HTTP proxy servers — including a built-in browser for free, publicly available proxies, organized by country.

## What Socks5 Relay – VPN & Proxy does

- **Connect** — route your device's traffic through a proxy you configure, over SOCKS5, SOCKS4, or HTTP.
- **Discover** — browse free public proxies by country, refreshed automatically.
- **Verify** — test any proxy's real-world connectivity and exit location before relying on it.
- **Save** — keep a personal list of favorite proxies for quick reuse.

## Our approach

We built Socks5 Relay – VPN & Proxy to be transparent about how it works and what it can't guarantee. Proxy connectivity — especially free, publicly sourced proxies — is inherently variable: servers go offline, ownership and trustworthiness vary, and performance isn't guaranteed. Rather than overpromise, we focus on giving you the tools to test and verify a proxy yourself before you rely on it, and on being upfront that free proxies are run by third parties we don't control.

## Our values

- **Transparency** — no hidden data collection; see our Privacy Policy page for specifics on what the App does and doesn't access.
- **User control** — your proxy credentials and preferences stay on your device.
- **Honesty about limitations** — we won't claim guarantees about third-party infrastructure we don't operate.
